Outreach Program · Last updated 24 August 2026

The short version. Outreach Program is a personal tool I built for my own job search. I am its only user. It reads my own Gmail to keep track of the jobs I have applied to, and it sends emails from my own account. It has no other users, collects nothing from anyone else, and sells nothing to anyone.

Who this is

Outreach Program is built and operated by Ajaykumar Balakannan as a private, personal project. It is not a commercial product, it is not offered to the public, and there is no company behind it. Contact: ajayport2@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses

The app requests the following Google account permissions, and only for accounts I own and personally authorise:

  • gmail.readonly: to read messages in my own job-search mailbox, so that application confirmations, rejections and interview requests can be recognised and counted automatically.
  • gmail.send: to send job-search emails from my own account, which I write and approve individually before anything is sent.
  • drive.readonly and spreadsheets: to read my own resume files and contact lists.
  • openid, email, profile: to confirm which of my accounts is connected.

What it does with that data

Email in my job-search mailbox is categorised (application received, rejection, interview request, assessment, and so on) and a small amount of information is stored: the sender, subject, a short preview, the company and role where those appear, and the time it arrived. This is used to produce a private dashboard of my own job search and to alert me when something needs a reply.

Message contents are processed by third-party model APIs (Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude) purely to sort messages into those categories. No message is used to train any model by these providers under their API terms.

Where it is stored, and for how long

Data is stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database and in local files on my own computer. It is not shared with anyone, not published, not sold, and not used for advertising. There is no analytics, no tracking and no third-party access.

I delete this data whenever I no longer need it, and the entire dataset can be destroyed by deleting the database.

People who email me

If you are a recruiter or employer who has emailed me, your message is stored the same way any email is stored in any inbox, plus a category label. If you would prefer I delete a message you sent, email ajayport2@gmail.com and I will remove it.

Google user data

Outreach Program's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never sold, never transferred for advertising, and never used for any purpose other than the personal job-search features described above.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google Account permissions, which immediately stops the app from reading or sending anything.

Changes

If what the app does changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
